# App Store submission draft

Name: Location Wrapped
Subtitle: Your places, your story
Suggested category: Lifestyle

## Description

Turn your recorded location history into a personal map and recap. Discover your most-visited places, see how your recorded time adds up, and share a visual summary.

• Explore a sample history before granting location permission.
• Optionally record locations in the background on iPhone.
• Import timestamped GPX or Location Wrapped JSON history.
• View estimated visits, recorded distance and monthly activity.
• Export your history or delete it from your device.

Location recording is optional. Background updates depend on iOS permissions and system conditions and can be interrupted. Visit durations and distance are estimates from available samples, not a complete record of every journey. History is processed on your device; map services and destinations you choose when sharing have their own privacy practices.

## Reviewer notes draft

The native app requires no account or payment. On first launch, complete onboarding and explore the demo. Demo statistics are sample data.

To test with real data: open Profile, turn Demo data off, and choose Capture current location once or Import GPX or JSON history. To test background recording, select Start background recording and grant foreground and Always location permission. Lock the phone and move between locations. Reopen the app to view saved samples. iOS controls frequency; visits require sufficient nearby samples, so a short test may show points without a stay.

Pause recording from Profile. Delete real location data stops recording and removes local recorded/imported history. Privacy policy and Support are available in Profile. Sharing is user-initiated.

Do not claim continuous force-quit tracking. Do not direct the reviewer to the access-controlled website as a substitute for the native app.
